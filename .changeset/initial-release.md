Initial release — sixteen typed client-side hooks: `ThemeProvider`/`useTheme` (class or `data-theme` attribute, configurable storage key), `useMediaQuery`, `usePrefersReducedMotion`, `useDebouncedValue`, `useClickOutside`, `useOverlayDismiss`, `useMountTransition`, `useCopyFeedback`, `useCookieConsent`, `useCountdown`, `useFormErrorScroll`, `useImagePreview`, `useObjectUrlMap` (leak-free — revokes from a ref on unmount), `usePageTitle`, `useScrollToTop`, and `useVisibilityChange`. Ships ESM + CJS with a `"use client"` banner.
